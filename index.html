Heritage & history

Tequila & tradition

Since 1937, the Camarena family has been crafting top-quality tequilas in Mexico. Today, Felipe Camarena and his sons continue this four-generation legacy with G4 Tequila.

G4 is made using traditional methods: hand-removing the cogollo, slow-roasting agave in stone ovens, and distilling in copper stills to maintain its natural character. El Pandillo is powered by solar energy and recaptured heat.

G4 Blanco was named top Blanco tequila of 2025 by the Beverage Testing Institute.

How this works

This is the PKGD Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed in place of the Bottle Nexus cart. The cart and checkout are Shopify. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

01Shopify cartShoppers add to cart on this site, in the native Shopify cart. No third-party cart and no redirect.
02Shopify checkoutThe one-page Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and card payments by AccelPay.
03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer in the buyer's state, or about 30 minute delivery through Gopuff where G4 is stocked.
04Your customer dataEvery order and customer record lands in the PKGD Shopify admin. You own the customer.
ReserveBarAlso on ReserveBar.com. G4 Blanco, Reposado, Añejo and La Madera are listed on ReserveBar.com and sell on Minibar.

Keep Shopify, add the AccelPay app

Same store, same theme, same Shopify checkout. The AccelPay Shopify app plugs in and takes over payments, taxes and order routing to licensed retailers, so there is no third-party cart and the customer data stays in your Shopify admin.

  • Ships to 40+ states through 3,500+ licensed retail locations
  • Instant delivery in about 30 minutes through Gopuff, plus DoorDash from local retailers
  • Live in about one week from kickoff, with no per-order charge to the brand
  • Licensed retailers set the final retail price

Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.

Reordering

Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
